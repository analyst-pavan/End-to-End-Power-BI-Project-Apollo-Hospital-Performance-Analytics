# 🏥 Apollo Hospital Management Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Apollo Hospital Management Analytics Dashboard** is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project developed using **SQL** and **Power BI** to analyze hospital operations, financial performance, patient demographics, and doctor performance. The project transforms raw healthcare data into meaningful insights through interactive dashboards, enabling hospital management to make informed, data-driven decisions.

The solution focuses on three major business areas:

- **Hospital Operations:** Monitor bed occupancy, available beds, maintenance status, ward utilization, and branch-wise performance.
- **Finance Analytics:** Analyze total revenue, expenses, profit, department-wise revenue, yearly financial trends, and top billing patients.
- **Patient & Doctor Analytics:** Evaluate doctor performance, consultation fees, patient demographics, admission trends, department-wise doctor distribution, and doctor ratings.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Improve hospital resource utilization.
- Monitor operational efficiency across branches.
- Analyze financial performance and profitability.
- Evaluate doctor productivity and patient care.
- Track patient admission trends and demographics.
- Support strategic decision-making with interactive dashboards.
